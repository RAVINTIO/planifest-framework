01

The short version

Framework shouldn't chase Zero's speed. Framework wins by being the delivery pipeline that a regulated organisation can defend to an auditor.

  • Three gaps let an agent weaken its own controls today. Fix them before anything else 0000297–0000299.
  • The bundled skill library has to go. Its provenance data is defective. Developers bring their own approved, licensed skills through overrides instead 0000301, 0000302.
  • Four base controls carry most of the audit story. They are attributable approvals, an audit trail, traceability, and one feature at a time. Most later entries build on them.
  • Jira becomes the system of record for planning and approval. A named human moves each gate, and Framework binds the approval to a commit and an artefact digest 0000295, 0000352.
  • Postmortems go further than Zero's. Zero writes good records and never closes them. Framework adds CAPA with effectiveness checks and a gate before archive 0000330–0000336.
  • Compliance frameworks become profiles. A small set of controls is on by default. GAMP 5, ISO 27001 mapping, and UK GCAP are opt-in 0000348, 0000356.
02

Both products share an ancestor and most of their hooks. They now serve different buyers. Zero optimises for throughput across many features. Framework optimises for control, evidence, and a clear line of accountability for every change.

04

Fix first: three urgent gaps

Each of these lets an agent weaken a control without a human seeing it. All three are small, self-contained Change Pipeline runs.

0000297

The write gate fails open

If design.md exists but has no parseable component paths list, the gate allows every write. That includes .claude/settings.local.json, which can switch off every hook.

path-rules.mjs, line 897 · confirmed
0000298

Fast Path can rewrite policy

The agent can authorise its own Fast Path writes to ravintio-overrides/. That folder holds the loop toggles, standing instructions, and push permission.

workflows/fast-path.md
0000299

Setup can leak a secret

When settings.json fails to parse, setup prints the parser's message. That message quotes part of the file, so a secret can reach the terminal and the agent's context.

setup.sh and setup.ps1 · 4 sites

06

Skills: remove, then govern

The bundled library holds 372 third-party skills. Removing it ends the redistribution duty for future releases, and past tags keep their attribution files. The provenance data shows why a regulated buyer couldn't accept the library as it stands.

The governed route for a team's own skills

After removal, ravintio-overrides/capability-skills/ is the only way in. Setup installs a skill only when every check passes. Agents can draft a provenance record but can never fill in the approver.

Prerequisite

Fix backlog 0000272 before 0000301. Otherwise the migration's setup re-run deletes developers' own skill folders.

07

One feature at a time, driven by Jira

One Jira Story maps to one feature, one run, one pull request, and one release. The agent moves work statuses. Only a named human can move a review status, and Jira's workflow enforces that through an approvers group that leaves out the agent's account.

What makes an approval stand up

  • The agent signs in to Atlassian as its own account, never the human's.
  • Jira's changelog holds the identity, and nobody can edit it.
  • A later gate re-hashes the artefacts. A changed file needs a fresh approval.
  • Schema and destructive approvals stay in the repository.

How one feature at a time holds

  • Before it claims a Story, the agent searches for any other active Story on its account and stops if it finds one.
  • The repository lock carries the Jira key, and the hooks check it 0000305.
  • If Jira can't be reached, no gate can pass. Unknown never counts as approved.
08

Postmortems that go further

Zero's postmortems are good records. They build an evidence timeline, ask five whys, and end at a process gap rather than a person. Three things stop them working as a quality system.

What Zero's records show

All 26 postmortems and retrospectives still read status: "open". Two stops got no postmortem until two days later. One lesson recurred in nine retrospectives and nothing noticed the pattern.

What Framework adds

Deterministic triggers and a gate before archive. A CAPA register where closure needs evidence that the action worked. Impact on validated state, deviations, incidents, trends, and periodic review.

09

GAMP 5 and the V-model

Framework already does much of what GAMP 5 asks. It has traceable requirements, maker-checker splits, test-first code, and verification by execution. What it lacks is the GxP vocabulary and the records that tie those steps into a validation story. The GxP profile maps Framework's artefacts onto the specification hierarchy.

Smallest defensible set

Attributable approvals 0000286, the GxP profile 0000337, risk-based test depth 0000339, the traceability matrix 0000340, and the validation summary report 0000342.

The AI pipeline is itself a tool to qualify

Auditors ask first for an intended-use statement for the agent. Entry 0000344 pins the model ID and Framework version per artefact, and packages the test suite as qualification evidence.

Source check

The GAMP 5 Appendix D9 and D11 and ISPE AI Guide references come from general knowledge. Each entry says to check them against the source text at pickup.

Other frameworks to assess

Entry 0000356 lists these for the same default, optional, triggered, or out-of-scope call. The Cyber Resilience Act and IEC 81001-5-1 matter most for Framework's target market.

EU and UK regulation

Cyber Resilience Act, NIS2, DORA, the EU AI Act with ISO/IEC 42001, GDPR Article 25, and Cyber Essentials.

Life sciences and devices

IEC 62304, ISO 13485, ISO 14971, IEC 81001-5-1, FDA section 524B premarket cybersecurity, and the EU GMP Annex 11 revision and draft Annex 22.

Finance and payments

SOX IT general controls, PCI DSS v4.0 requirement 6, and SOC 2.

Industrial and automotive

IEC 62443-4-1, ISO/SAE 21434, and UNECE R155. Automotive safety standards need a tool qualification level that an LLM can't reach.

US government

CMMC 2.0, CISA Secure by Design, and the secure software self-attestation form.

Supply chain

SBOM formats (SPDX and CycloneDX), OpenSSF Scorecard, OpenChain (ISO/IEC 5230 and 18974), and CIS Controls control 16.
